## Gap lifetime / right censoring

At the first >5s valid-frame break, expiry or available-history boundary, stop observing and censor the opening. As-of horizon tolerance is 2s. Gaps below .1 pp remain in response analysis but are excluded from fractional lifetime estimates. Use event/day-weighted Kaplan–Meier for 25/50/full closure; full means sign crossing or ≤.01 pp residual. Doubling-before-half uses competing-risk cumulative incidence, not independent alarms. 64 block resamples estimate uncertainty when follow-up supports it. No follow-up is MISSING, not a failed closure. Estimates assume noninformative censoring; source failures and expiry may violate that assumption.

## Actual structure payoffs and ordinary entry

Every terminal/touch candidate now uses actual long call/put or vertical payoff at option expiry. H is evaluated at event cutoff; option expiry can be later. Model E[X|H] and E[X|not H], then PM substitutes the event mixing probability. Conditional GBM shape uses existing flat IV/zero carry; event mass is calibrated to the displayed options Q. Q-only value must agree with the actual quote band within numerical uncertainty. No p × maximum-payout vertical EV remains. Debit/max payout is descriptive only.

PM information value = (P−Q) × (payoff-if-H − payoff-if-not-H). Entry spread/fees/slippage and estimated exit drag are separate. Candidates test nearby strikes and one/two/four-strike widths, with positive net information/net-EV priority then information/drag ratio. A PM-helped expression whose drag outweighs its information may still be tracked and is explicitly labeled negative net EV. Q-only model residuals cannot make it eligible. Exit drag is a current-book estimate, not a forecast of future spreads; expiry EV is separately retained.

Historical entry freezes exact legs at the first eligible event/day opening, then uses the first valid source receipt at/after opening+5s (or next XNYS open+10s for closed equities), with a fixed ten-minute wait limit. Natural exits are fixed at 30m/2h, session close−1ms, next-open first valid receipt after open+10s, and recorded expiry price where available. Fixed-time exits use quotes available by the deadline. No post-outcome exit optimization or new-strike substitution.
