# BASIS RSI forward protocol v4

The authorized experiment lasts thirty calendar days from its recorded mandate start, subject to actual Guest Pass access. The local experiment journal is the continuous record; its first account observation is the permanent performance baseline. Existing tapes, stars, wallets, and exploratory reports remain evidence from their own periods.

Research and execution sessions use the BASIS and thinkorswim graphical interfaces. Every trading session verifies the visible paperMoney identity. Reuse the signed-in browser tab. Quotes inherit the venue's actual delay label; retrieval time is never represented as exchange time. BASIS CLI work supports engineering, historical experiments and the evidence journal. No order submission API is added.

The operator's October 9 objective amendment makes **the highest observed paperMoney portfolio equity during the original thirty-day window** the primary score. Keep final equity, realized/unrealized P&L, costs and drawdown visible as descriptive results. Preserve the original $100,000 baseline and mandate dates. A peak includes open-position broker marks and must come from a captured GUI account observation; option quote marks, retrospective estimates and observations after the original end do not raise the score. The true unsampled intraday peak remains unknown. This metric is a reporting measure, not a validated peak-maximizing strategy.

The same amendment supersedes the initial 0.5% per-position / 2% aggregate preservation caps for future entries. Use actual simulated buying power and the operator's requested half-Kelly sizing, with exact contract payoffs, costs and correlation considered in each frozen plan. Half Kelly is model-dependent expected-log-growth sizing; it does not itself optimize the maximum equity reached during thirty days. Record uncertainty and the full sensitivity range rather than claiming an identified edge or a peak-optimal allocation. Initially prefer defined-risk structures. The UI payoff audit now assesses affordability against the latest reported option buying power under the activated amendment; that budget is not an order quantity recommendation. Prior plans and results retain their original limits and versions.

Holding periods follow the BASIS hypothesis and its payoff/repricing horizon. There is **no default thirty-minute exit or blanket end-of-day liquidation**. An expiry-based payoff model needs an expiry-compatible holding hypothesis; an earlier exit requires recorded signal invalidation, changed executable economics, a declared repricing target, a stated horizon or an actual venue constraint. Freeze the review schedule, exit conditions and horizon before entry. Amend a still-open position prospectively with explicit evidence and versioning if circumstances change. Do not silently change its thesis after a loss, and do not reopen the completed first pilot simply to erase its exit.

Before entry, freeze a BASIS-derived PLAN with captured signal/source IDs and clocks, model and strategy versions, exact contract legs and expiry, executable venue quotes, quote delay, estimated costs, maximum loss, the testable hypothesis, entry criteria and exit conditions. A positive displayed EV alone is insufficient: examine monitoring/resolution semantics, model sensitivity, actual quote calibration, latency and the empirical evidence. Reject an economics-invalid quote or an incompatible product mapping. Deribit contracts do not authorize an assumed ETF/futures substitute.

Protocol v3 permitted one initial forward **calibrated-book pilot** to obtain actual execution feedback before deploying a validated trading rule. That pilot is completed and its frozen plan remains historical evidence. Its single long-option contract type had to fit its individual captured bid/ask under every predeclared scenario, and the PM bid/ask range had to retain the hypothesized event-gap direction. Positive information after all stressed friction and numerical margins remained unresolved; the pilot cannot be reported as a robust strategy entry or proof of edge. The operator's pretrade instruction to use half Kelly superseded the earlier 0.05% equity cap and one-contract quantity. Five calls were sized from an expiry-payoff reference and exited under the agent's thirty-minute rule. That horizon mismatch and the actual loss remain in the journal. This is execution evidence, not a test of the full expiry thesis. The short timer is not carried into v4. General strategy entries retain the full sensitivity requirements below.

Forward paperMoney fills are FILL events carrying actual broker fill IDs where exposed, structure quantities, exact contracts, gross cash per structure and actual fees. When the GUI does not expose the external identifier, `experiment-journal-1.1` records an explicit null, `NOT_EXPOSED_IN_GUI` status and a unique local evidence reference; it never manufactures a broker ID. Statement and filled-row evidence establish the actual fill. An unfilled order is ORDER evidence, not performance. MARK evidence records an executable quote-based liquidation estimate and its quote status, separate from actual fills. Unknown fees are null; later confirmed costs append COST evidence linked to the original fill, preserving the original observation. FIFO realizes partial closes. Account observations supply equity and buying power; drawdown is explicitly sampled, not continuous.

For each meaningful revision record the Git revision, motivating evidence, historical tests, forward activation time, and subsequent results. Exploratory samples are not forward performance. Keep rejected hypotheses, missed opportunities, adverse outcomes and prior versions. Report performance by signal class and model/strategy version, compare with no trade, and add simple alternative benchmarks only with clearly stated quotes and timing. Never reset the baseline or revise earlier source Q values to improve a result.

`experiment-journal-1.2` keeps objective amendments in append-only REVISION evidence and the agent's current activity, finding, next action, strategy version and actual coverage in STATE evidence. BASIS displays the latest effective objective and work state beside actual filled positions and the sampled equity peak. At each resumed session read this state first, verify the broker account, inspect BASIS Markets/details/graphs/stars, and append the decision and next action before ending. Capture account equity at session start/end, after fills and material position changes, and during active monitoring so peaks are observed rather than reconstructed. Explicitly record unavailable or usage-limited monitoring; do not imply unattended coverage.

Iteration 1 adds evidence accounting and diagnostics. `quote-audit-1.0` inverts OTM bid/mid/ask prices under zero-carry European GBM, then compares the current continuous touch Q with XNYS regular-session monitoring. Prices still evolve through nights/weekends; those intervals cannot resolve a regular-session touch. Brownian bridges integrate within sessions. Monte Carlo error describes numerical sampling only. American pricing, carry, tenor, variance allocation, Pyth reference prices and physical versus risk-neutral differences remain limitations. This diagnostic is not an activated replacement probability model or a trading edge.

Iteration 2 adds `payoff-audit-1.0`, an on-demand research report for regular-session equity touch signals. Independent GBM paths evolve until actual option expiry; session bridges determine event mass only until cutoff. A discounted analytic European price anchors each vanilla leg. PM reweighting keeps the model's conditional payoff shapes and changes only hit/no-hit mass, so its information value remains a hypothesis. Each leg must fit its own actual bid/ask (half-cent quote-rounding tolerance); offsetting pricing errors cannot produce a calibration pass.

Before examining results the sensitivity set is fixed: OTM bid, mid and ask IV inversion with uniform calendar variance; mid IV with 50% and 75% of expiry variance in regular sessions; and a mid-quote carry stress with annual continuous rate 5% and dividend yield 0.5%, re-inverting the same quotes. Rates and variance shares are unfitted stress assumptions, not market estimates or a calibrated confidence band. Test the entire captured PM bid/ask interval, current-book round-trip friction, conditional effective samples and two Monte Carlo SE. A candidate must survive every scenario and the activated affordability budget. A model/book failure makes its EV inadmissible. Candidate coverage is long options and debit verticals; other expressions are not ruled out by their absence. Passing sensitivity checks does not establish clock alignment, predictive information, or an executable edge. The original reducer, stars, and adverse marks retain their versioned definitions.

The bridge survival calculation follows the standard conditional endpoint formula, also described in [Google's tf-quant-finance Brownian bridge implementation](https://github.com/google/tf-quant-finance/blob/master/tf_quant_finance/black_scholes/brownian_bridge.py). Tests compare one-session hit probabilities against the reflection-principle solution and discounted vanilla values against analytic prices under carry and alternate variance clocks.

The persistent agent goal retains the mandate across continuations. It does not guarantee the computer, browser login, or dispatcher will remain available for thirty days. At each resumed session read the local journal and this protocol first, record unavailable access, and proceed with independent research/engineering when execution access is missing. Do not fabricate unattended sessions or fills.

At the end produce every trade/fill/cost/outcome, supported scientific findings and remaining uncertainty, the engineering record and tests, the best supported functioning revision, and an evaluation separating demonstrated progress from hypotheses.
